## Full Description
Sakhi (meaning "female companion" in Sanskrit) is a beautiful, private menstrual cycle tracker that blends modern cycle science with the timeless wisdom of Indian Ayurveda.

**What makes Sakhi unique?**
Unlike other period trackers, Sakhi maps each phase of your menstrual cycle to its corresponding Ayurvedic dosha — giving you personalized food, herb, yoga, and lifestyle recommendations rooted in 5,000 years of holistic health wisdom.

**4 Cycle Phases, Beautifully Color-Coded:**
- Menstrual Phase (Vata) — Rest, warmth & nourishing foods
- Follicular Phase (Kapha) — Energy, creativity & light eating
- Ovulation Phase (Pitta-Kapha) — Peak energy & cooling foods
- Luteal Phase (Pitta→Vata) — Grounding, introspection & warming herbs

**Features:**
- Color-coded calendar showing all your cycle phases at a glance
- Cycle wheel visualization with your current day marker
- Ayurvedic food recommendations (Ahara) for each phase
- Herbal remedies (Aushadhi) — Shatavari, Ashwagandha, Triphala & more
- Yoga asanas & pranayama suited to your current energy
- Daily rituals (Dinacharya) honoring each phase
- Mood & symptom tracker with emoji check-ins
- Fertile window indicator
- Cycle insights — days to next period, cycle length tracking
- Works offline — no internet needed
- 100% private — all data stays on your device

**Your Privacy Matters:**
Sakhi stores everything locally on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking, no ads. Your cycle data never leaves your phone.
