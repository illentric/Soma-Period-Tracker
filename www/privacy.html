🪷 Soma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Overview

Soma ("the App") is an Ayurvedic period tracking app. Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is handled.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects only the information you voluntarily enter:

  • Period start date
  • Cycle length and period duration
  • Daily mood and symptom check-ins

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device only. We use your browser's localStorage (and on native apps, the device's local storage). No data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not serve advertisements

5. Health Data

The menstrual cycle data you enter is considered health-related information. This data never leaves your device. We do not integrate with Apple Health, Google Fit, or any external health platform.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Reset" button in the App, or by clearing your browser/app data through your device settings. Since no data is stored remotely, deletion is immediate and permanent.

8. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services, APIs, or analytics platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: Shailja Dubey — privacy@soma-app.com
